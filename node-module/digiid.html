Node module

Digi-ID sign-in

Installed: Digi-ID is on for this arcade

With this module installed, the white-label website in front of this arcade lets people sign in by scanning a QR code with a DigiByte wallet (DigiByte Go, or any Digi-ID app) instead of typing a password. Uninstall it to turn Digi-ID off; passwords keep working either way.

For the people who use your site

  1. Sign in with their @handle and password, as always (accounts from ARCADE Legacy too).
  2. Open Wallet › Digi-ID and choose Turn on Digi-ID in this browser, then scan the code with their DigiByte wallet.
  3. From then on, the sign-in bar in that browser offers Digi-ID: one scan, no password, and the wallet opens ready to sign.

Why it's safe

  • Digi-ID proves who is signing in; it never sees or holds coins.
  • Turning it on keeps a sealed copy of the wallet in that browser, under a key made of two halves: the browser keeps one, the site keeps the other and hands it back only after a scan from the linked Digi-ID. Neither can open the wallet alone, and the site never sees the twelve words.
  • It's per browser: a new browser signs in with the password once, then can turn Digi-ID on too. Anyone can turn it off in one browser or everywhere.

For operators

The site (arcadesite) asks this arcade for its installed apps (apps.installed) every 30 seconds and turns Digi-ID on when a node module named Digi-ID is installed. The site setting "digiid": "on" or "off" overrides it. Digi-ID codes name the site's own address, so serve it over https (a plain-http test address works too, marked u=1).
