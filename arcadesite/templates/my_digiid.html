Built on Digi-ID, created by the DigiByte community. ARCADE ID is Digi-ID carried to every chain an ARCADE wallet holds: the same codes, so a DigiByte wallet such as DigiByte Go works as it is, and any ARCADE wallet can answer with its Pepecoin, Dogecoin, Litecoin, Bitcoin or DigiByte key, among others.
